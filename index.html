Overview
Gendai Katana LLC was founded by a group of young industrial designers and mechanical engineers who passionate about katana culture.

By combining 3D printing technology with modern design aesthetics, Gendai Katana breaks through the limitations of traditional forging to create katanas that are affordable for everyone. Beyond preserving the spirit and symbolism of the katana, we challenge conventional perceptions with bold creativity, striving to find a new balance between “tradition” and “innovation,” and allowing the katana to shine once again in the modern era.

I was commissioned as a freelance product designer and UX developer to create an e-commerce website, customer panel for signed in users , and admin panel for personnel at the company from the ground up. This exciting project allowed me to design and technical expertise to deliver products with seamless and delightful digital experience. signed in users

Problem and challenge
  • This is a young company with many competitors in same field, the primary challenge is to differentiate itself from competitors with product design and to break through in this highly competitive market.
  • In the absence of physical stores, they need to convince customers to transact with them online , 2D images are still far insufficient.
  • Most e-commerce websites have complicated user login system and checkout process, which causes inconvenienc and even leads to customers churn.
  • Many consumers who is aged or have vision problems complain that the fonts size the company use are too small, and the poor color contrast which they can hardly read, ultimately leading them to give up.
My approach
Color contrast comply WCAG level AA or above
Sign in with Google account
Interactive 3D content
Streamlined check out process

Color contrast comply WCAG level AA or above
The contrast between background and foreground color is fundamental for accessibility design, especially for those who are aged or have vision problems.

So WCAG compliance is the first thing to consider for the UI design, and I believe that more accessible UI design can definitely attract more customers.

Sign in with Google account
Our memory is pretty limited, and it is never easy to memorize multiple usernames and passwords used to sign in to a platforms.

In addition, they have to deal with the trivial matters of daily life and work, so forgetting passwords is a common issue.

Therefore, reducing cognitive load for customers is necessary and shall be prioritized.

According to my survey data, everyone has at least one Google account, and some people even have more than two, and this is why I propose to leverage Google account sign in.

After the website was launched, the result of data analysis shows that more than 70% of customers are satisfied with this design.

Interactive 3D content
Through competitor analysis, I find out that most e-commerce websites, whether direct or indirect competitors, they showcase their product details with photos.

This approach seems normal, but it also brings another problem. When consumers view photos, they can only zoom in to see details, and cannot learn more details by rotating the photos 360 degrees.

This is what interactive 3D contents can do, with it, customers can zoom and rotate the 3d content 360 degrees. They can draw and sheath the katana by sliding the slider and the thumb.

Consumers can learn more detailed information, thereby increasing their willingness to buy. And this is the innovative way to differ the company from their competitors in the field as well.
The Streamlined check out process
The complicated check out process is always annoying for everyone, so it must be Streamlined.

I propose to reduce some unnecessary forms and multiple stages of process with few forms such as full name, complete address, and the phone number, in order to reduce the cognitive load for customers.

After discussion with stakeholders, they find that this proposal is technically feasible.
